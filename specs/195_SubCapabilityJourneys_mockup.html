Sub-capability rows cover descendants at any depth, ordered by target period (undated last). A descendant's current journey shows when planned, in flight, or done — abandoned journeys never appear. The "Part of" line lists every ancestor with an active journey, nearest first. Both sections are pure read-side derivation: nothing is stored, and no write affordances exist on either.
